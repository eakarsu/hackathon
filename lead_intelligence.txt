4. Core Definitions
Open Leads: NEW, QUALIFIED, QUOTED.
Partition Rule: DUE + OVERDUE = Open Leads (mutually exclusive).
DUE
* NEW and CreatedDATEONLY > NEW_CUTOFF
* QUALIFIED/QUOTED and (followUpDate > DUE_CUTOFF OR appointmentDate > DUE_CUTOFF)
OVERDUE
* NEW and CreatedDATEONLY <= NEW_CUTOFF
* QUALIFIED/QUOTED and (followUpDate <= DUE_CUTOFF OR IS NULL) AND (appointmentDate <= DUE_CUTOFF OR IS NULL)

5. Lead Intelligence Generation
For each associate: - Count of Open, DUE, OVERDUE leads. - List of DUE tasks. - List of OVERDUE tasks. - Recognition for SOLD in last 7 days.
Prioritization
1. OVERDUE ? oldest first.
2. DUE ? nearest upcoming task first.
3. Appreciation ? SOLD leads in last 7 days.
Messaging
* Tone: clear, supportive, action?oriented.
* Appreciation: Congratulate SOLD wins.
* OVERDUE: Warn explicitly.
* DUE: Recommend next action.
